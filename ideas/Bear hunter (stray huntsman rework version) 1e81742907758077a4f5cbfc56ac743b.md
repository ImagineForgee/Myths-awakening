# Bear hunter (stray huntsman rework version)

**Core Stats:**

- **High Health:** Designed to be a durable challenge. Suitable for extended fights (e.g., 200–300 HP).
- **High Melee Damage:** Punishes mistakes severely (e.g., 12–20 damage per hit).
- **Movement Speed:** Moderate (approx. 3 blocks/second). Capable of chasing but not uncatchable.

---

### **Weapons & Abilities**

### **1. The Executioner’s Axe**

- **Role:** Close-range punisher.
- **Behavior:** If the player is within close range (~3 blocks), the boss switches to the axe and performs powerful, slow-charged attacks.
- **Damage:** Very high; encourages players to stay at mid-range or kite.
- **Environmental Interaction:** Can break blocks (especially leaves/wood) around it if obstructed (e.g., stuck in trees or tight spaces).
- **Combo Mechanic:** If the player tries to back away after being hit, the boss quickly follows up with a second hit or switches to harpoon to pull them back in.

### **2. Harpoon Pike (with Chain Pull Mechanic)**

- **Role:** Anti-cheese tool and gap closer.
- **Range:** Long (8–10 blocks).
- **Behavior:** Fires a chain that hooks the player. Upon hit:
    - Pulls the player directly toward the boss, bypassing terrain (phases through soft blocks or gaps).
    - Cancels cheesing tactics like pillaring, walling, or water traps.
    - If successful, briefly stuns the player and automatically triggers an axe strike follow-up.
- **Cooldown:** Moderate (e.g., 8–12 seconds).
- **Anti-Cheese Design:** Prevents ranged stalling and terrain abuse.

### **3. Ice Bombs / Smoke Bombs**

- **Role:** Disruption and anti-ranged deterrent.
- **Behavior:** Throws bombs when taking too many ranged hits in succession.
    - Creates a **smoke/ice fog** area (radius 6–8 blocks) that obscures vision.
    - Player can still land hits if using effects like **Glowing**, **Spectral Arrows**, or **Invisibility counters**.
    - Encourages pre-fight planning and disables long-range camping.
- **Synergy:** Used in tandem with the Harpoon to punish ranged spam.

### **4. The Pike (Default Weapon)**

- **Role:** Primary weapon for general combat.
- **Range:** Slightly longer than a sword (e.g., 4.5 blocks).
- **Attack Speed:** Moderate; designed to make players think about spacing and timing.
- **Behavior:** Used in mid-range combat. Encourages players to dodge and outmaneuver rather than face-tank.

### **5. War Horn (Mid-Range Catchup Tool)**

- **Role:** Short dash / chase ability.
- **Behavior:** If the player is between 5–8 blocks away (not far enough for harpoon, not close enough for melee), the boss blows a horn:
    - Dashes forward quickly (3–4 blocks).
    - Buffs movement speed for a brief period (2 seconds).
    - Can chain into an attack if it closes the gap.

### **6. Jump/Mobility Escape**

- **Role:** Terrain recovery.
- **Behavior:** If the boss detects itself as "stuck" (e.g., in a pit, surrounded by terrain), it leaps upward (~5 blocks) and forward to reposition.
- **Cooldown:** Long (15–20 seconds); only used reactively.

---

### **Design Goals:**

- **Counter-Cheese:** Harpoon and Ice Bombs prevent players from avoiding fair combat.
- **Dynamic Combat:** Switches tactics based on distance and player behavior.
- **Encourages Strategy:** Players must mix ranged and melee, manage positioning, and use special items to overcome fog and pull effects.
